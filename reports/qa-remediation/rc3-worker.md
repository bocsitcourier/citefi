# RC3 Worker Remediation — `FIXED_UNVERIFIED`

## Scope and root cause

This change addresses text-quality promotion at production delivery boundaries.
The original article worker could log a failed final Guardian audit and then
save that best-effort output as `COMPLETE`. It also treated
`GPT4_ENHANCED`/`CHATGPT_REVIEWED` as terminal duplicate states, allowing an
incomplete checkpoint to evade the remaining finalization path. The final
article output check did not inspect anchor/Markdown link destinations.

The social worker and manual social-variant regeneration had a deterministic
caption pass, but lacked a required final composition of the evidence-backed
claim reviewer and applicable brand-policy result. Orchestrator failures were
advisory in these paths. Batch/campaign exports also accepted intermediate
article checkpoints.

## Implemented changes

- Added `lib/generation-finalization-gate.ts`, a no-repair delivery gate that
  composes existing `article-output-safety`, `social-validation`,
  `contentReviewService`, and brand-policy functionality.
  - Structural output defects reject before a judge/provider call.
  - The existing claim reviewer is invoked with `useJudge: true`; rejection or
    reviewer unavailability fails closed.
  - Applicable policy is loaded and executed. Campaign content requires its
  same-team immutable snapshot. A legacy non-campaign item remains supported
  until it has a completed usable team profile.
  - Exact policy prohibitions/disclaimers are evaluated deterministically.
    Factual-claim validity remains with the existing evidence-backed reviewer;
    this change does not invent a broad claim-word blacklist.
- Added article URL destination validation in
  `lib/article-output-safety.ts`, including unsafe schemes and malformed
  HTML/Markdown destinations while retaining safe relative/fragment links.
- Wired the gate immediately before each article `COMPLETE` write, including
  speed mode. Guardian final rejection now throws the typed quality error,
  retaining the already checkpointed GPT output rather than promoting it.
  Duplicate completion recognizes only `COMPLETE`.
- Wired the social gate after the existing final caption/URL compliance pass in
  both the queue worker and manual variant regeneration. Rejection occurs
  before `READY`; the existing failure handler retains diagnostic variant
  content and marks it failed.
- Added fatal `QUALITY_GATE_FAILED` error taxonomy. The existing pipeline
  handler therefore performs its normal final-failure release/cap-cancellation
  path rather than retrying provider generation. It does not alter immutable
  provider usage/COGS records or task-179 receipts.
- Export routes now require `COMPLETE` (not intermediate checkpoints), a
  nonempty final article body, and output-safety validation.
- Updated the critic loop’s missing-policy check so policy is mandatory for
  campaigns, not legitimate profile-less legacy non-campaign content.

No new repair/re-generation path was introduced. In particular, no failed
checkpoint, image, or other paid media is rewound or regenerated by this
change. Existing bounded repair stages were not invoked.

## Limitations / approval boundary

`FIXED_UNVERIFIED`: this work intentionally made no live provider, network,
database-write, billing, media, export-download, or workflow-restart call.
The tests use injected/mocked final reviewers and pipeline billing dependencies
for the negative settlement assertion. A principal/architect must approve
before any staged/live run; no task-179 receipt or provider-ledger mutation was
made.
