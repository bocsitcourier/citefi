---
name: Free article product policy
description: The owner's chosen preview/signup/paid boundaries and why approval remains separate.
---
One free article starts before signup. Signup unlocks that same full article in a watermarked reading view, not another excerpt. Application copy, download and export require a paid subscription. Do not promise that a website can prevent screenshots, browser inspection or all copying.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose full watermarked reading after signup rather than preview-only access. This conversion path must not silently bypass the existing approval and authentication policy.

**How to apply:** Keep pending-account reading scoped to its private trial capability. Ordinary workspace access and checkout still require approval and normal sign-in. Do not wipe previously issued balances when changing the new free offer.
