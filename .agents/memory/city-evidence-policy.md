name: City marketing evidence
description: Evidence boundaries for local marketing and safe Census geography matching.
---
City pages address small-business needs across major US cities. Publish only genuine, verified reviews and consented customer photographs; never imply a local office, customer, endorsement or result from a city landing page alone. Population is context, not reachable demand.

Lead public city pages with business problems, relevant industry examples and customer buying journeys—not Census/population statistics. Geography data can support directory coverage behind the scenes, but is not the marketing proposition.

**Why:** Place statistics do not demonstrate business value. The site's standing positioning is customer questions and actual buying decisions, not demographic reports.

**How to apply:** Explain how a local business answers questions before a call, booking or purchase; provide a concrete next step into the real article flow. Do not replace absent reviews with invented proof.

Image accessibility and AI-search metadata must describe actual visual content, not infer a photograph's location from its city landing page. Licensed stock imagery is not customer evidence or Citefi-created photography.

**Why:** Reusing representative photos across city pages can otherwise turn page targeting into false local or authorship claims.

**How to apply:** Keep stock-source attribution separate from the site's publisher identity; omit unverified image locations and creators. Never move private customer media into public image metadata or sitemaps.

**Why:** The owner requested actual people and local reviews, but supplied no verified testimonials or permissions. Official Census data also distinguishes similarly named places: Saint Paul and Saint Paul Park are different geographies, so prefix aliases can silently corrupt rankings and capital status.

**How to apply:** Match the complete official place name plus state when refreshing local data; preserve the official label and source vintage. Census CSVs may use CP1252 rather than UTF-8. Keep unavailable estimates explicit instead of inventing values or claims.
