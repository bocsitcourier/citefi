---
name: Full-platform marketing positioning
description: Owner's standing requirement for comprehensive, buyer-oriented marketing beyond the article entry offer.
---
Position Citefi as the full multi-format marketing workspace, not merely an article-writing tool. One free article is the entry offer; public selling pages must explain images, social content, podcasts, video and the supporting campaign/journey/team work where relevant.

**Why:** The owner repeatedly rejected pages that omitted those formats or failed to answer small-business buying questions. They want the site to match customer needs and guide buyers toward choosing a plan, not just discuss safeguards.

**How to apply:** Audit public acquisition changes for the buyer's problem, inputs, usable outputs, workflow, review/setup, budget/plan fit and next action. Tailor industry/city examples to actual customer decisions. Continue source-backed gap checking during implementation; do not substitute imaginary testimonials, outputs, local evidence or performance promises for missing proof.
